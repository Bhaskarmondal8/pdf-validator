# AdSense preparation

Google AdSense requires site review and policy compliance before ads can show.

Before applying, publish useful original pages such as:
- How PDF digital signatures work
- How to verify e-Aadhaar signatures safely
- Why a PDF may show an unknown certificate
- Privacy and data handling
- Terms of use and contact page

Do not claim the site is an official UIDAI/Adobe service.
Do not make fabricated government-verification claims to attract clicks.
Do not ask visitors to click ads.

After approval, add Google's official AdSense script/slots to the site.
